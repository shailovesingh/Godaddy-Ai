## 📊 Prompt Quality Guidelines

When creating prompts for best results, include:

### ✅ Good Prompt Elements
- **What**: Core product or service
- **Who**: Target audience
- **Where**: Location (if relevant)
- **Why**: Unique value proposition
- **How**: Key differentiators

### ❌ Avoid
- Extremely vague descriptions
- Multiple unrelated business ideas
- Offensive or inappropriate content
- Excessive length (500+ words)

### 📏 Ideal Length
- Minimum: 20 characters
- Recommended: 100-300 characters
- Maximum: 500 characters
